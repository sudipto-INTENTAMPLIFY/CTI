## Trust / procurement
`You are paying for intelligence coverage, evidence processing, account context, activation infrastructure, integrations and operating expertise.`

`We do not sell fabricated intent or promise a predetermined number of SQLs, meetings, opportunities, pipeline or revenue.`

`Website engagement and pricing interest are signals—not sales qualification. Qualification remains governed by agreed ICP, role, account, need, timing and CRM criteria.`

## Qualification form
Required commercial fields only:
- company
- work email
- role
- target market
- primary GTM objective
- approximate target-account volume
- current demand/pipeline challenge
- desired timeline
- approximate program/budget range
- current CRM / intent stack
- consent

Submission does not equal MQL or SQL.

## Routing contract
`website_interest -> pricing_intent -> qualified_buying_signal -> human_or_crm_validation -> mql_or_sql_only_when_approved_criteria_are_met`

Verified Identity and Marketing Permission remain independent states.

## FAQ
**How is pricing determined?** `By target-account coverage, intelligence depth, activation requirements, integrations, markets and operating support.`

**Do you guarantee SQLs or revenue?** `No. CTI structures evidence and activation around agreed qualification criteria; commercial outcomes depend on market, offer, sales execution and buyer behavior.`

**Does submitting the form make me an SQL?** `No. Website and pricing engagement are intent signals. Sales qualification requires approved qualification evidence and human/CRM validation.`
