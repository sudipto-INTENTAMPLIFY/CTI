# CyberTech Intelligence — Public Pricing Page Spec

Status: RC11 candidate specification; public-market iteration only.
Date: 2026-08-13

## Evidence boundary
This iteration deliberately excludes CTI internal commercial collateral, CRM data, private analytics, private customer evidence, and internal pricing material. It is based on public market evidence only. The figures below are recommended public list-price anchors, not verified CTI historical realized pricing or margin economics.

## Recommended exact public pricing stack

| Tier | Public list price | Primary KDM fit | Commercial job |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Open Intelligence | $0 | CISO, security leader, researcher, marketing/GTM evaluator | Establish trust and category familiarity |
| Signal Intelligence | $15,000/year | Demand Gen, ABM, RevOps, security-vendor GTM | Prioritize target accounts and market/buyer signals |
| Account Activation | $36,000/year | VP Marketing, Demand Gen, ABM, Growth | Convert intelligence into coordinated account activation |
| Pipeline Intelligence | $72,000/year | CRO, CMO, RevOps, Sales/Marketing leadership | Connect signals, activation, qualification context and measurement |
| Enterprise GTM Intelligence | $120,000/year | CRO/CMO, business-unit leader, global GTM | Multi-market intelligence, governance, integrations and strategic operating support |

Public display should use exact annual list prices for the first four paid anchors and `$120,000/year` as the enterprise base price, followed by `Enterprise scope priced after discovery` for materially larger deployments. Do not advertise a guaranteed SQL, meeting, pipeline or revenue quantity.

## Why KDMs can accept this ladder

The public competitive market already establishes a credible buying band around these anchors. Common Room publicly lists Essential at $2,500/month billed annually ($30,000/year). UserGems publicly lists Core at $33,000/year, Advanced at $69,000/year and Elite at $120,000/year, plus implementation fees. Demandbase publicly describes a platform fee plus flat per-user fee with customized plans. CIENCE publicly lists a $5,000 setup, $2,000/month strategic team and $499/month platform before optional SDR capacity. Belkins advertises appointment-setting programs from about $4,000.

These benchmarks support a CTI position above commodity enrichment/automation and between buyer-intelligence software and fully managed GTM execution. The $36K, $72K and $120K anchors sit near already-publicly-established enterprise GTM buying thresholds, while $15K creates a lower-risk paid entry point.

## KDM acceptance by buying context

### Cybersecurity / technology vendors
Strongest fit. CMO, CRO, VP Demand Gen, ABM and RevOps leaders can evaluate CTI as a specialist intelligence + activation operating layer rather than generic lead generation. Trust language is especially important: current cybersecurity market research reports a substantial vendor-trust gap, making evidence, transparency and independent validation commercially material.

### Financial services / insurance / regulated industries
Likely fit where buying teams require traceable evidence, governance, account prioritization and stakeholder context. Lead with evidence quality, data provenance, controls and integration—not volume promises.

### Healthcare / life sciences
Likely fit for complex enterprise selling where committees, compliance and long buying cycles make account context valuable. Position around prioritization, buying-group context and measurable activation; avoid implying regulated-data capabilities unless separately verified.

### Industrial / manufacturing / infrastructure
Moderate-to-strong fit for high-ACV, named-account selling. Position around target-account intelligence, market context, executive activation and multi-stakeholder buying rather than high-volume demand generation.

### SaaS / enterprise software / professional services
Strong fit where the customer already funds ABM, RevOps or sales-intelligence tooling. The public market demonstrates willingness to pay approximately $30K–$120K annually for adjacent buyer-intelligence platforms, so CTI must differentiate through specialist intelligence and managed activation.

### SMB / transactional / low-ACV markets
Weakest fit for the paid stack. A $15K annual floor requires sufficient ACV, target-account value or strategic-market importance. Route smaller evaluators toward Open Intelligence rather than discounting the operating tiers into commodity territory.

### Trust / procurement section
H2: `Know what you are buying.`

`You are paying for intelligence coverage, evidence processing, account context, activation infrastructure, integrations and operating expertise.`

`We do not sell fabricated intent or promise a predetermined number of SQLs, meetings, opportunities or revenue.`

`Website engagement and pricing interest are signals—not sales qualification. Qualification remains governed by agreed ICP, role, account, need, timing and CRM criteria.`

### Commercial scaling disclosure
H2: `How pricing scales`

`Account coverage × intelligence depth × activation depth × integration complexity × operating support.`

Use the following form ranges:
- Target accounts: `Under 250`, `250–1,000`, `1,000–5,000`, `5,000+`
- Annual program investment: `$15K–$35K`, `$36K–$71K`, `$72K–$119K`, `$120K+`

### FAQ
**Why publish pricing?**  
`To give buying teams a credible budget range before discovery and reduce unnecessary sales friction.`

**Are these subscriptions or lead packages?**  
`Annual programs. Pricing is based on intelligence and operating scope, not cost per lead.`

**Do you guarantee SQLs or revenue?**  
`No. CTI can structure evidence and activation around agreed qualification criteria, but commercial outcomes depend on the market, offer, sales execution and buyer behavior.`

**Can enterprise scope exceed $120K?**  
`Yes. $120,000/year is the Enterprise base price. Larger account universes, regions, integrations and operating requirements are scoped after discovery.`
